------------------------------  Artificial Intelligence  -----------------------------

AI is the technology that enables machines to perform intelligent tasks like learning,
reasoning, and decision-making. AI is widely used in daily life through smartphones, 
voice assistants, and recommendation systems. It improves efficiency, saves time, 
and helps in solving complex problems in fields like healthcare, education, and 
transportation. AI is shaping the future, but its responsible use is necessary to 
avoid risks like privacy concerns and job loss.
